    How it works

    1. Drop your files

      Files or a whole folder. Up to 10 MiB without an account, more when you sign in.

    2. Encrypted on your device

      Files are encrypted in your browser before upload. The key travels in the link, never to our servers.

    3. Share the link and PIN

      Send the PIN through another channel. Links expire after download or within a week.
