Frequently asked questions

The useful details about limits, expiration, and how your files stay private.

What are the size limits?

Anonymous browser sends are limited to 10 MiB per file. The CLI supports larger files, folders, and live streaming. Install it with curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/xreal/drop2/main/scripts/install.sh | bash.

How long does a link stay available?

You choose when creating a share: delete it after the first completed download, or keep it for one day, two days, or one week. Expired shares are removed automatically.

Who can read my files?

Only someone with the complete share link can decrypt a file. If you enable PIN protection, they also need the separately shared PIN. Encryption and decryption happen on your device; drop2 stores ciphertext only.

Do I need to use a PIN?

No. PIN protection is enabled by default but can be turned off under Advanced options. The full link still contains a strong, random decryption capability and should always be treated as a secret.

What about misuse or abuse?

drop2 is for legitimate private file transfer, not public content hosting. Cross-share probing and repeated incorrect PIN attempts are rate-limited. Shares that violate acceptable use can be removed without inspecting their encrypted contents.

Can I send larger files?

Yes. The open-source drop2 CLI sends larger files and folders as live or stored encrypted shares. Browser account tiers and larger uploads are planned but are not available yet.
